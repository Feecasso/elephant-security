Engagement process

How support works

A straightforward process designed to keep the work understandable, documented, and proportionate to the size of the environment.

  1. 01

    Find out what matters

    Understand the devices, users, tools, constraints, and the problems that need to be solved first.

  2. 02

    Assess and explain

    Identify issues and risks in plain language, then agree on priorities before changes are made.

  3. 03

    Fix, harden, and document

    Complete the agreed work, verify the result where possible, and record what changed and what needs attention next.

  4. 04

    Support what is sustainable

    Recommend ongoing support only when the environment, tools, and agreed procedures can actually support it.

Working principle

If a request is outside scope or technically unrealistic, it will be stated clearly.

  • Scope agreed before work begins
  • No guaranteed claims
  • Changes documented
  • Plain-language recommendations
